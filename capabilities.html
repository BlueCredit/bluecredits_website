Capabilities

Translating complex hydrologic, engineering, regulatory, and public health requirements into practical, audit-ready programs.

BlueCredit Water Consulting provides independent, science-based water sustainability and risk management services to public agencies, healthcare institutions, utilities, and industrial operators. We translate complex hydrologic, engineering, regulatory, and public health requirements into practical, defensible water stewardship programs that protect human health, ensure compliance, and strengthen long-term water resilience at facility and watershed scales.

Water Sustainability Strategy

  • Corporate and institutional water stewardship program design grounded in basin context and operational realities
  • Water-positive and watershed replenishment strategies aligned with AWS, CDP, GRI 303, and SBTN
  • Disclosure, verification, and assurance support for regulatory compliance and ESG reporting

Water Resources Engineering Services

  • Watershed-scale hydrologic modeling integrating stormwater, surface water, streamflow networks, and groundwater
  • Quantification of water savings, reuse, replenishment, recharge, and efficiency impacts using science-based methods
  • Independent verification and audit-ready documentation at facility, utility, and watershed scales

Institutional Water Management

  • Water Management Plan development, audits, and lifecycle updates
  • Legionella risk assessment and prevention planning aligned with ANSI/ASHRAE 188 and CDC guidance
  • Cooling tower and domestic water system hazard analysis and operational oversight

Water Projects Management

  • End-to-end management of water projects from planning through implementation
  • Scope development, schedule and budget control, and multi-stakeholder coordination
  • Oversight of pilots, phased deployments, and performance verification

Digital Water Platforms

  • GIS-based water data monitoring, analytics, and watershed mapping
  • Dashboards, trend analysis, and technical reporting
  • Automation of stormwater, nutrient, and mitigation workflows for utilities and basin managers

Science-based water sustainability, engineering, and digital water management services for public agencies, utilities, and industrial operators.
