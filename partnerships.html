Strategic Collaboration

Partnership models

We collaborate with organisations to design, implement, and verify science-based water projects across a full spectrum of engagement types.

Technology Partners

Integrate IoT monitoring, remote sensing, and analytics. Co-develop tools and pursue joint research initiatives.

NGO & Community

Collaborate on watershed restoration, water access, capacity building, and local impact measurement.

Government & Institutional

Deliver water engineering and advisory under federal, state, and municipal procurement. SAM.gov registered.

Research Collaboration

Co-develop hydrologic methodologies, advance water data science, and publish peer-reviewed impact research.

Data Sharing

Exchange watershed data, satellite imagery, and monitoring datasets to improve basin-level modeling accuracy.

Subcontract & Teaming

Available for prime or subcontractor teaming on federal, state, and municipal water projects.
