Industries we serve

Supporting water-intensive and water-dependent sectors with science-based strategies that address operational risk, regulatory compliance, and long-term water resilience.

Data Centers

  • Water risk assessment for hyperscale and colocation facilities
  • Cooling system water balance and efficiency analysis
  • Water-positive and watershed replenishment strategy development
  • Support for disclosure, permitting, and community engagement

Municipalities & Utilities

  • Water supply, demand, and infrastructure resilience assessments
  • Stormwater, recycled water, and watershed project development
  • Rate, affordability, and long-term capital planning support
  • Regulatory compliance and public reporting support

Manufacturing

Textile, chemical, and food & beverage operations with diverse water quality, quantity, and discharge profiles.

  • Process water use assessment and efficiency opportunities
  • Water reuse, recycling, and discharge management strategies
  • Facility-level water risk and compliance assessment
  • ESG reporting and assurance readiness

Mining

  • Water balance modeling for mine sites and associated watersheds
  • Tailings, dewatering, and groundwater interaction assessment
  • Reclamation, closure, and watershed restoration planning
  • Independent verification and disclosure support

Science-based water sustainability, engineering, and digital water management services for public agencies, utilities, and industrial operators.
